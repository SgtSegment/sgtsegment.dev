# General Privacy Policy for my video games

I do not collect any data, however if the game is **online multiplayer**, it uses **Epic Online Services** and requires some communication with **EOS** servers, which will collect data according the the **Epic Games privacy policy**, located at **https://legal.epicgames.com/epicgames/privacy-policy**.
Within my EOS implementations, I only utilize networking (Peer-to-Peer connection systems) and achievements (if the game is played through Epic Games).

## Meta Horizon
As stated prior, I do not collect any data. However, if the game is a Virtual Reality game that is distributed through the Meta Horizon store, along with being subject to EOS privacy policies, **it is also subject to Meta privacy policies**, located at **https://www.meta.com/legal/privacy-policy/**.<br>
Multiplayer games through Meta Horizon may authenticate using your display name, IP address, or other unique identifiers to your Meta Horizon account, meaning your Meta Horizon display name may be shown to other players playing through Epic Online Services.
